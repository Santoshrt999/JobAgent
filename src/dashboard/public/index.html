    Designed and Engineered by Atresh Somayajula

    JobAgent, a local-first application cockpit binds to 127.0.0.1, never exposed nothing is submitted without you

    Add a job by URL

    Paste a public job posting URL from any career site. It is read, scored against your profile, and added to the board. Sites that block automated reads are reported honestly.

    Search job boards

    Search the job boards for any role, whether or not it has anything to do with your profile.

    Results are kept in their own list, labelled with the role you searched for. They never appear on your personalized board, and running this can never change what Find new jobs returns — your profile, target titles and scoring are left exactly as they are.

    Uses the query-first sources only (Workable, and Adzuna if you have a key), which answer in seconds rather than the ~14 minutes a full profile run takes.

    Record your submission

    With a confirmation number this is recorded as submitted. Without one it is recorded as reported, pending verification - never silently counted as applied.

    Replace the application URL

    Paste the direct employer or ATS application URL. Aggregator links (LinkedIn, Indeed, Dice…) cannot be auto-filled reliably, so they are rejected here.
